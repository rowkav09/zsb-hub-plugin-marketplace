Converter

Turn inventory and capsules into tokens through configurable steps.

What it does

Converter can sell cases and eligible skins, buy and open capsules, and optionally use accumulated tokens for Dragon Lore upgrades. Individual stages have their own controls.

How it uses the farmer

Uses cash and price limits, previews in Practice, and publishes which accounts are assigned to its upgrade step so Armory can skip them.

Publisher catalog

The signed catalog lists version 1.4.0, which requires core 1.0.6. Install or update publisher plugins from the app’s Plugins tab.
