What it does

Effects manages quantities and ammo reserves. A queue waits for the current effect in a family to finish, then activates the next configured effect.

How it uses the farmer

Uses account-scoped inventory and effect state. This version was present in the inspected beta and absent from the signed catalog snapshot.
