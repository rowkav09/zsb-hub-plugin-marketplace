What it does

Games models the expected token cost of reaching the next reward tier and tracks bets, rewards and net results. The XP mode targets Armory progress. Actual results can vary.

How it uses the farmer

Uses routed requests for some games and sockets for crash and blackjack. Reads the Armory plan and avoids accounts being seated in battles.

Publisher catalog
